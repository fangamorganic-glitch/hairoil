Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Thank you for shopping with Fangam Organic! We are committed to delivering your favorite organic hair oils, shampoos, ubtan, soaps, hair masks, and serums safely and promptly to your doorstep.

1. Order Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days (excluding Sundays and public holidays) after receiving your order confirmation. You will receive a notification once your order has been dispatched.

2. Delivery Time & Charges

  • Standard Delivery: Typically takes 3 to 5 business days across the country.
  • Shipping Charges: Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and order weight. Free shipping offers may apply during special promotional periods.

3. Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive your tracking details via WhatsApp or SMS so you can easily monitor the status of your parcel right up to your delivery address.

4. Delayed or Missing Shipments

While we partner with reliable courier services to ensure timely deliveries, unforeseen circumstances (such as severe weather conditions or local public holidays) may occasionally cause minor delays. If your delivery takes longer than expected, please feel free to reach out to us.
