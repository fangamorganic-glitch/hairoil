Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Got questions about Fangam Organic products? Find detailed answers below!
1. What makes Fangam Organic products different from commercial products?

Fangam Organic products are 100% natural and free from harsh synthetic chemicals, sulfates, parabens, and artificial fragrances. We use authentic herbal formulations infused with pure plant extracts and traditional herbs to nourish your skin and hair naturally.

2. How should I apply the Fangam Organic Hair Oil for the best results?

For optimal results, gently massage a generous amount of hair oil into your scalp and hair roots using your fingertips. For deep nourishment, leave it overnight (or at least 2-3 hours before washing). For young children, it is recommended to apply it during the day for a few hours rather than leaving it overnight.

3. Which natural herbs and ingredients are used in your Hair Oil?

Our hair oil is power-packed with time-tested traditional herbs like Amla, Reetha, Shikakai, Bhringraj, Brahmi, and pure botanical carrier oils that synergistically strengthen hair roots, control hair fall, and stimulate healthy growth.

4. Can Fangam Organic Hair Oil help with severe hair fall and bald patches?

Yes! Regular use of our nutrient-rich herbal oil reactivates dormant hair follicles, improves scalp blood circulation, significantly reduces hair fall, and promotes baby hair growth over consistent weeks of application.

5. Are your herbal shampoos safe for chemically treated or colored hair?

Absolutely. Our herbal shampoos are completely sulfate-free and gentle on the hair cuticle. They cleanse the scalp effectively without stripping away your hair's natural moisture or fading color treatments.

6. How often should I use the Herbal Shampoo and Hair Mask?

We recommend washing your hair 2 to 3 times a week using our herbal shampoo. For dry or damaged hair, apply our intensive repair Hair Mask once a week after shampooing to lock in deep hydration and silky smoothness.

7. What are the benefits of using Fangam Organic Traditional Ubtan?

Our traditional ubtan is an exquisite blend of pure roots, grains, turmeric, and floral extracts. It gently exfoliates dead skin cells, removes tan, unclogs pores, and gives your face a radiant, natural glow without any chemical irritation.

8. How do I use the Ubtan for daily skincare?

Mix a tablespoon of ubtan with rose water, milk, or yogurt to form a smooth paste. Apply it evenly on your face and neck, let it dry for 10-15 minutes, and gently scrub it off with lukewarm water while rinsing.

Yes, our handmade natural soaps are enriched with plant oils and nourishing butter. They are crafted to be extremely gentle, making them suitable for all skin types, including sensitive and dry skin.

No! We take pride in maintaining 100% purity. Our formulations contain zero mineral oils, zero artificial colors, and zero parabens.

12. Can men use Fangam Organic products?

Yes, all our products—including hair oils, shampoos, soaps, and ubtan—are unisex and formulated to deliver exceptional personal care results for both men and women.

Store your organic oils, serums, and jars in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Ensure caps and lids are tightly closed after every use to preserve the potency of natural herbs.
