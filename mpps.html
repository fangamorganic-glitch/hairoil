Key Benefits

Revitalizes damaged hair follicles, stops premature hair thinning, controls frizz instantly, adds a natural radiant gloss, and promotes healthy natural growth.

How to Use

Massage gently onto the scalp for 5 minutes using fingertips. Leave for 2 hours or overnight before washing with a mild organic shampoo. For daily protection, apply 3-4 drops along the length of your damp or dry hair.
