Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

At Fangam Organic, we take utmost care in preparing and packaging our organic hair oils, shampoos, ubtan, soaps, hair masks, and serums. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase.

1. Returns & Exchange Eligibility

Due to the personal care and organic nature of our products, we generally do not accept returns unless:

  • You received an incorrect item that does not match your order.
  • The product packaging was damaged, leaked, or tampered with upon delivery.

If you encounter any such issue, you must notify us within 48 hours of receiving your order.

2. Conditions for Return

To be eligible for a return or exchange, the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and in the same condition that you received it.

3. Refunds

Once your returned item is received and inspected, we will notify you via WhatsApp or email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit or bank transfer will be issued within a specific timeframe.

4. Non-Returnable Items

Opened, used, or partially consumed bottles, jars, or packets of hair oils, shampoos, ubtan, soaps, or serums cannot be returned or refunded for hygiene and safety reasons.
