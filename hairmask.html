How to Use

Tailored recipes according to your scalp type

Oily Scalp

Mix as needed with plain water or rose water to form a smooth paste.

Normal Scalp

Mix as needed with lukewarm water for natural softness and glow.

Dry Scalp

Mix as needed with lukewarm water, yogurt, or a few drops of natural herbal oil.

Dandruff / Itchy Scalp

Mix as needed with lukewarm water, yogurt, and a few drops of fresh lemon juice.

Application Directions

Step 1: Soak the mixture for 30 minutes prior to application to activate herbal nutrients.
Step 2: Apply thoroughly onto scalp and hair roots, leave for 30 minutes, then rinse thoroughly with plain water or mild organic shampoo.
