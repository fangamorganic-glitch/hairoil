THE ART OF HERBAL EXTRACTION

Artisanal Small-Batch Making Process

Unlike mass-produced chemical shampoos made with harsh industrial detergents, every batch of Fengam Organic Herbal Shampoo is crafted through four deliberate botanical stages.

01

Herbal Selection & Sun-Curing

Hand-picked organic Amla, Shikakai, Reetha, Brahmi, Rosemary, and Neem leaves are carefully sorted, washed, and naturally sun-cured to concentrate their active phytonutrients.

02

Slow Botanical Decoction

The whole herbs undergo low-heat simmering in triple-purified water for hours, gently drawing out vitamins, saponins, and minerals without scorching delicate antioxidants.

03

Cold-Infusion & Mild Plant Cleansers

Once cooled, the herbal infusion is blended with coconut-derived gentle surfactants, pure Aloe Vera juice, and Mulethi—with zero SLS/SLES, zero parabens, and zero silicones.

04

pH Balancing & Cold-Fill Bottling

Each batch is micro-filtered, calibrated to match the scalp's natural pH (~5.5), and cold-filled into protective bottles to preserve peak botanical potency.

SPECIALIZED CARE GUIDE

How to Use Sulphate-Free Shampoo

Botanical Lather Note: Because Fengam Organic Herbal Shampoo is 100% free from synthetic sulphates, it creates a rich, creamy botanical emulsion rather than harsh chemical bubbles. This gentle lather cleanses deeply while keeping hair hydrated.
  • 1 Thoroughly Wet Hair: Saturate your hair and scalp completely with lukewarm water for 30–60 seconds to open hair cuticles.
  • 2 Emulsify in Palms First: Pour a small amount into wet palms and rub your hands together with a splash of water to activate the creamy herbal lather before applying.
  • 3 2-Minute Scalp Massage: Massage gently with your fingertips for 2 minutes so the Amla, Rosemary, and Neem extracts absorb into the roots.
  • 4 Rinse Cool: Rinse thoroughly with cool or room-temperature water to seal the hair cuticle and lock in natural shine.
